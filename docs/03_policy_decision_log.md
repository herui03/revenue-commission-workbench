# 03 · Policy & rule decision log

The commission policy here is **invented for the demo**. It was chosen so the mechanics a Revenue
Operations analyst must explain (splits, tiers, clawbacks, late data, close) are all visible. It is
not an industry standard, legal requirement or any employer's plan. Each decision lists the options
considered and the consequence, so the choice can be defended — or changed — in an interview.

| # | Decision | Alternatives considered | Why this one | Consequence / trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P-01 | **Cash basis**: commission is earned on `COLLECTION` events, not bookings or invoices | Pay on booking; pay on invoice | Cash is the least reversible signal and makes refunds meaningful | Bookings and invoices are shown for context only. Long collection cycles delay pay |
| P-02 | **Money = integer minor units**; rates in basis points; exact integer/Fraction arithmetic | Python `float`; `Decimal` everywhere | Integers cannot drift; bps keeps every rate exact | Rates finer than 0.01% cannot be expressed (deliberate) |
| P-03 | **One rounding, half-up, per line** (after summing bracket portions) | Banker's rounding; round each bracket; round per statement | Easy to explain on a statement; one visible rounding delta per line | Sum of rounded lines can differ from rounding the sum by < 0.5¢ per line (shown as `rounding_delta`) |
| P-04 | **Split cents by largest remainder**, ties to the smaller rep id | Round each share; give residual to the largest split; random | Credited cents always equal source cents; deterministic; order-free | A 1¢ tie always favours the alphabetically smaller rep id (documented, test HC-07) |
| P-05 | **Marginal monthly ladder per rep × month × currency**: up to the threshold at base rate, excess at accelerator | Retroactive ("whole month at 8% once you cross"); quarterly ladder | Marginal is fair at the boundary (no cliff) and explainable line by line | Earlier lines never change when a later collection crosses the threshold |
| P-06 | **Attainment = gross positive credited collections** | Net of refunds | Keeps a refund from silently re-tiering unrelated deals | A refund does **not** restore tier capacity (HC-10) |
| P-07 | **Refunds reverse the stored original earning cumulatively**: after refunds totalling R of a collection x, total reversal = `half_up(E × R / x)` | Recompute at today's rate; reverse each refund with its own rounding | Uses the rate the rep was actually paid; path-independent; full refund reverses exactly E (HC-08: 10¢ in 3 parts → exactly 10¢) | Needs the original line — held if the original is itself on hold/pending/excluded |
| P-08 | **Refund credit** allocated by each rep's *not-yet-reversed* credit (largest remainder) | Re-apply split %; cumulative largest remainder | Conserves every refund to the cent, never reverses more than was credited, and a full refund returns each rep to exactly zero | Partial-refund credit per rep can differ by 1¢ from the naive split % (HC-16) |
| P-09 | **Currencies are separate**; no FX | Convert to a reporting currency | FX would add a rate-source question the demo cannot answer honestly | Two ladders per rep if they sell in SGD and USD (HC-12) |
| P-10 | **Plan versions immutable, month-aligned, chosen by the cash event date**; "latest effective_from ≤ date" wins | Mid-month versions; mutable end dates | A month never mixes two rate cards; history is append-only | Plan changes land on the 1st of a month. A version may not start in or before a closed month (`RETROACTIVE_PLAN`) |
| P-11 | **Assignments effective-dated; latest start wins**; same rep + same start date = ambiguous → rejected | Overlap = error; mutable end dates | Lets a rep move plans without editing history | `ASSIGNMENT_AMBIGUOUS` at import; engine also holds defensively |
| P-12 | **Splits immutable per contract** once loaded | Effective-dated splits | Keeps the credit trail simple | A split change after cash needs a manual adjustment (documented limitation) |
| P-13 | **Close = freeze the exact reviewed run**. Close recomputes; any difference in lines, holds, exclusions, totals, controls, KPIs or inputs → `STALE_REVIEW` | Close whatever is current; close stored run and list differences | Guarantees "what was reviewed is what is frozen" (reviewer finding R-2) | A booking-only import after review forces a re-review |
| P-14 | **Late data → blocking hold → reviewer posts it into the first open month** (or excludes it) with a reason | Reopen the closed month; auto-post silently | Closed statements are never restated; every late item has an owner and a reason | Late cash is tiered on the **closed month's frozen ladder** at that month's plan version (HC-13), not re-tiering frozen lines |
| P-15 | **Late items post only into the first open month** | Any open month | Guarantees an original is posted no later than its refunds | One posting target, visible on the hold |
| P-16 | **Periods close in order**, only after the month has ended | Any order | Frozen ladders and refund history stay consistent | `EARLIER_PERIOD_OPEN`, `PERIOD_NOT_ENDED` |
| P-17 | **Manual adjustments**: reason ≥ 10 chars, open months only, append-only, own line type | Edit source rows; free-form overrides | Never disguised as source earnings; reversible only by an opposite entry | Shown separately on statements and KPIs |
| P-18 | **Negative expected payout** is recorded as-is (net recoverable) | Automatic carry-forward to next month | Carry-forward/draw rules are plan-specific and out of scope | Documented limitation |
| P-19 | **Variance = recorded − expected**, per rep × month × currency; cases keep id/owner/notes; changed variance after resolution reopens | Recompute cases from scratch | Investigation history must survive reruns (R-3) | "Resolved" means explained, never "paid" or "corrected" |
| P-20 | **IDs are uppercase `[A-Z0-9_-]{1,40}`**, never normalised; composite keys hashed as JSON arrays; line keys use `:` which IDs cannot contain | Case-insensitive IDs; `a|b` string keys | Two source systems' IDs can never silently merge or collide | Lower-case source IDs must be fixed upstream |
| P-21 | **Supported years 2000–2099** for dates and periods | Unlimited | Rejects `0000-01`-style input instead of crashing (R-5) | Documented range |
| P-22 | **Actors are labels**; preparer ≠ reviewer checked on labels only | Real authentication | A local demo must not pretend to enforce segregation of duties | Stated on every page footer and in the SOP |
