Policy & glossary

One invented demo policy, chosen to make the mechanics visible. It is not an industry standard or a legal rule.

The four money words — never mixed

Booking
A signed contract's value, dated by its booking date. Shown for context; earns nothing here.
Invoice
A bill sent to the customer. Not modelled; the invoice reference on a cash row is informational only.
Cash collected
Money actually received (a COLLECTION event). The only commission basis. A refund returns part of one specific collection.
Commission
What the calculation says a rep has earned: earnings, clawbacks, late (prior-period) adjustments and manual adjustments. Expected payout is their net. Recorded payout is what the (synthetic) payout register says. None of these is recognised revenue.

Rules

  1. Split credit. Each contract has one credit split totalling exactly 100.00%. Cents are split by largest remainder (ties → smaller rep id), so credited cents always add up to the collected cents.
  2. Marginal monthly accelerator. Per rep × calendar month × currency: credited cash up to the threshold earns the base rate; cash above it earns the accelerator rate. Cash is walked in date then id order, so file order never matters.
  3. Rounding. Exact integer arithmetic; one half-up rounding per line.
  4. Refunds. Reverse the stored original earning in proportion to the cash refunded, cumulatively (after refunds totalling R of a collection x: reversal = half_up(E × R / x)), so a full refund reverses exactly E. Refunds do not reduce attainment or touch other deals.
  5. Plan versions. Immutable, month-aligned; the version effective on the cash date applies. A new version cannot start in a closed month.
  6. Close. Draft → review (preparer label) → close (a different reviewer label). Close freezes the exact reviewed run; any later change makes the review stale.
  7. Late data. Cash dated in a closed month waits on a blocking hold. A reviewer posts it into the first open month (tiered on the closed month's frozen ladder at that month's rates) or excludes it — with a reason.
  8. Currencies. SGD and USD are always separate; there is no FX.
